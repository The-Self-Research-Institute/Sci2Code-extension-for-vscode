# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Introduction

At Self Research Institute, we are committed to fostering a welcoming, inclusive, and collaborative community. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for contributors to ensure that our open-source projects remain a positive and productive space for everyone.

## Diversity Declaration

We are committed to providing a welcoming, safe and inclusive environment for all, regardless of:
gender and gender identity.
- Age.
- Sexual orientation.
- Disability.
- Physical appearance and body size.
- Race and ethnicity.
- Religion or worldview.
- Nationality and social origin.

## Standards of Behavior

- Respect: Treat all community members with respect and courtesy.
- Constructive Communication: Use clear, courteous and inclusive language. Give and receive constructive criticism.
- Collaboration: Strive to understand and solve problems together. Support the development of others.
- Professionalism: Behave professionally in public and private environments related to the project.
- Inclusiveness: Strive to create an environment that is accessible to all, encouraging participation from diverse groups.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment and Discrimination: Any form of harassment, intimidation, discrimination or bullying is unacceptable.
- Obusive Language and Images: Using language or sharing images that are offensive, vulgar or degrading.
- Personal Attacks: Personal attacks, offensive comments about individual characteristics or experiences.
- Unethical Actions: Plagiarism, falsification of information, violation of copyright or other intellectual property rights.
- Harassment: Repeated unwanted comments or contact, stalking, unwanted behavior of a sexual nature.

## Conflicts of Interest.

Members of the community should avoid situations where their personal, professional or financial interests may conflict with the goals of the project. If there is a potential conflict of interest, it should be disclosed to the appropriate individuals or teams in the project.

## Reporting Concerns

If you experience or witness behavior that is inconsistent with this code, you are encouraged to report it:

- Directly: Contact a member of the moderation or administration team.
- Anonymously: Use available tools or forms to report incidents anonymously.

All reports will be taken seriously and treated with confidentiality. The Code Enforcement team is committed to taking appropriate action.

## Enforcement

Violations of the Code of Conduct may result in various disciplinary actions, including:

- Warning: A private or public warning regarding the violation.
- Temporary Suspension: Temporary restriction of participation.
- Permanent Exclusion: Permanent removal from the community and blocking access to project resources.

Enforcement decisions will be made by the moderation team in accordance with established procedures.

## Summary

All community members are required to abide by this Code of Conduct. We strive to create an environment where everyone feels respected and can contribute freely. Thank you for your cooperation and support in promoting these values.

## Acknowledgments

This Code of Conduct is inspired by the Contributor Covenant and adapted to align with our mission as a nonprofit organization.
